# Apothecary

A small interactive piece from my portfolio. Tell the crystal ball what you seek, pour two ingredients into the cauldron, stir three times with the ladle, and it brews a potion illustration you can keep as a phone or desktop wallpaper.

**Try it:** [julianaholly.com/apothecary.html](https://julianaholly.com/apothecary.html)

## What's in it

- Six potions: Love, Confidence, Courage, Luck, Calm and Creativity
- Six ingredients on the shelf, to drag or tap into the cauldron
- A ladle you stir by hand, with a brew that takes the colour of what you poured
- Each potion as a phone wallpaper (1290 × 2796) and a desktop wallpaper (2560 × 1440)
- Works with mouse, touch and keyboard, and respects the "reduce motion" setting

## How it was made

I'm a brand designer, not a developer. I designed and art directed the piece, and built it by directing Claude (Anthropic's AI) to write the code, testing each version and sending it back with notes until it felt right.

- **Concept, design and art direction:** Juliana Holly
- **Illustration:** the Love potion is drawn by hand in Illustrator and Photoshop. The other five potions were made from it with AI image tools, under my direction, to match its style.
- **Code:** written with Claude. One HTML file, plain CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks.

## Run it

Download the folder and open `index.html` in a browser.
