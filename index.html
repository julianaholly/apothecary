Apothecary

Brew thyself a potion

Hark, wanderer, and draw near the glass. Name what thy heart seeketh, choose two ingredients from the shelf, and stir the cauldron thrice about. It shall brew thee a potion of thine own, to carry with thee and to keep.

II · The ingredients

Choose thy ingredients

Choose two bottles: click one, or drag it to the cauldron, and it pours itself in. The glowing ones are what the glass foresaw for thee. Then take hold of the ladle and stir the brew round three times.
